# Quimbaya Gold

> Category: Heritage & Luxury Hardware
> High-contrast museum exhibition system for pre-Columbian gold artifacts,
> parametric physical CAD models, and technical storytelling.

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Curatorial, solemn, luminous. The viewer steps into a vault bathed in black granite, illuminated by warm 3200K optical framing projectors catching gold specular facets.

## Color Palette & Roles
- **Background:** `#08090B` (Obsidian Vault deep black)
- **Foreground:** `#F3F4F6` (High contrast crisp white)
- **Accent (primary):** `#F5C242` (Quimbaya Gold, Au 82.5% alloy)
- **Accent (specular):** `#FFF2A8` (Golden lustre, facet gleams)
- **Accent (secondary):** `#C25B38` (Sacred terracotta, ritual contrast)
- **Surface (card):** `#111317` (Subtle warm slate)
- **Surface (elevated):** `#181B22` (Interactive hover container)
- **Border:** `rgba(245, 194, 66, 0.16)` (Gold hairline stroke)
- **Success/Live:** `#10B981` (Emerald green, CI/CD telemetry)
- **Muted:** `#9CA3AF` (Secondary documentation)
- **Dim:** `#6B7280` (Micro telemetry, metadata)

## Typography Rules
- **Display / Titling:** `'Cinzel Decorative'`, `'Syne'`, `'Cinzel'`, serif/geometric sans with capital tracking (`letter-spacing: -0.02em` to `0.06em`).
- **Headings & Technical Labels:** `'Space Grotesk'`, sans-serif, modular numerical hierarchy.
- **Body & Prose:** `'Plus Jakarta Sans'`, sans-serif, line-height 1.65 for deep curatorial reading.
- **Telemetry / CAD Code:** `'JetBrains Mono'`, monospace for coordinate systems, dimensions, JSON payloads.

## Layout Principles
- 12-column adaptive layout, 1240px container max-width.
- Asymmetrical hero layout: Left text heavy & structured, Right 3D viewport canvas.
- Real-time interactive inspection cards with active tab switching.
- Embedded telemetry HUD directly over the canvas plane.

## Do's and Don'ts
- **DO:** Use gold as deliberate luminous focus. Emphasize physical attributes (235 mm, 777.7 g, alloy ratios).
- **DO:** Render working 3D geometry with backface culling and directional Lambert shading.
- **DON'T:** Use generic AI purple/blue neon glows.
- **DON'T:** Use blurry floating circles without purpose.
